# Curses

If the host enables **Take All inflicts a curse**, taking the whole hand also applies one of these.

Take All still only leaves you with **one** curse. **Return to Sender** can stack a second onto someone else.

Toggle individual curses under **Toggle Cards -> MulliganMadness**.
