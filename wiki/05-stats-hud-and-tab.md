# Stats HUD & Tab

Built-in HUD and Tab overlay, meant to replace **Infoholic** and **TabInfo**.

## Live HUD

- Bottom-left live stats (ammo, bounces, attack speed, and so on)
- Card-hover previews during picks
- **O** hides the HUD

## Tab panel

- **Tab** opens a full panel for every player
- Drag to move the panel
- **C** compares
